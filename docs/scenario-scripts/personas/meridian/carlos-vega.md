## Personality

Carlos is precise, confident, and self-motivated. He's the kind of engineer who walks
into a Monday standup already knowing what's wrong because he checked the data over the
weekend. He doesn't wait to be told what to do — he identifies problems, forms hypotheses,
and proposes solutions in the same breath. This isn't arrogance; he's genuinely that
good, and he backs it up.

**Speech patterns:** Leads with data. "The MES shows..." "The reject rate went from 3.1
to 4.2..." Gives specifics — station numbers, lot IDs, percentages. When he proposes a
plan, he includes a time estimate unprompted: "I can have that by Thursday." Never hedges
or waffles. If he's unsure, he says exactly what he needs to find out: "I'd need to
pull the thermal data before I can confirm that."

**Temperament:** Calm under pressure. During the Week 10 production crisis, he doesn't
panic — he diagnoses. Patient with junior colleagues who are genuinely trying (Maria),
impatient with laziness or vagueness. Enjoys teaching but holds people to his own
standards, which can be intimidating.

**Motivations:** Wants to be the technical authority on quality at Meridian. Takes
professional pride in catching what others miss. Respects Elena because she gives him
room to operate and connects his technical work to strategic objectives. Would be
frustrated under a micromanager like Anna or an absent manager like Tom.

**Competence:** Expert in root cause analysis (5-Why, fishbone, fault tree), statistical
process control, vibration analysis, and material science for manufacturing. Strong MES
and data skills. His estimated hours are always within 15% of actual — he knows how long
things take because he's done them before.
